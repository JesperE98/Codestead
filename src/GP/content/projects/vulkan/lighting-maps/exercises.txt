=== task: ex-invert-specular
--- file: shaders/lighting_maps.frag
--- find:
    vec3 specular = ubo.light.specular * spec * texture(specularMap, fragTexCoord).rgb;
--- replace:
    vec3 specular = ubo.light.specular * spec * (1.0 - texture(specularMap, fragTexCoord).rgb); // the wood shines, the steel not

=== task: ex-emission-mask
--- file: shaders/lighting_maps.frag
--- find:
    vec3 emission = texture(emissionMap, fragTexCoord).rgb;
--- replace:
    // only on the wood, where the specular map is (nearly) black
    float onWood = texture(specularMap, fragTexCoord).r < 0.1 ? 1.0 : 0.0;
    vec3 emission = texture(emissionMap, fragTexCoord).rgb * onWood;

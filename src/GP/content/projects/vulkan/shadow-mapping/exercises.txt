    outColor = vec4(lighting, 1.0);

=== task: ex-lighter-shadow
--- file: shaders/shadow_mapping.frag
--- find:
    vec3 lighting = (ambient + (1.0 - shadow) * (diffuse + specular)) * color;
--- replace:
    vec3 lighting = (ambient + (1.0 - 0.5 * shadow) * (diffuse + specular)) * color;

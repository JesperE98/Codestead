=== task: ex-lighter-shadow
--- file: shadow_mapping.hlsl
--- find:
    float3 lighting = (ambient + (1.0f - shadow) * (diffuse + specular)) * color;
--- replace:
    float3 lighting = (ambient + (1.0f - 0.5f * shadow) * (diffuse + specular)) * color;

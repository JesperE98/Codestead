=== task: ex-disc
--- file: computeShader.hlsl
--- find:
    imgOutput[texelCoord] = value;
--- replace:
    // a white disc of radius 100 texels in the middle of the texture
    if (distance(float2(texelCoord), float2(400.0f, 300.0f)) < 100.0f)
        value = float4(1.0f, 1.0f, 1.0f, 1.0f);
    imgOutput[texelCoord] = value;

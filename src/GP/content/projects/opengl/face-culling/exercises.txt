=== task: ex-clockwise
--- file: main.cpp
--- find:
    glFrontFace(GL_CCW);    // front faces are the counter-clockwise ones (the default)
--- replace:
    glFrontFace(GL_CW);     // front faces are the clockwise ones: the cubes' outsides now count as back faces

=== task: ex-cull-all
--- file: main.cpp
--- find:
    glCullFace(GL_FRONT);   // cull the front faces: now only the back faces (the cubes' insides) are drawn
--- replace:
    glCullFace(GL_FRONT_AND_BACK); // cull every face: no triangle is drawn at all

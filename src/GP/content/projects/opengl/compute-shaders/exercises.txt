=== task: ex-disc
--- file: computeShader.comp
--- find:
    imageStore(imgOutput, texelCoord, value);
--- replace:
    // a white disc of radius 100 texels in the middle of the texture
    if (distance(vec2(texelCoord), vec2(400.0, 300.0)) < 100.0)
        value = vec4(1.0);
    imageStore(imgOutput, texelCoord, value);
